           INTERVIEW Q&A PREPARATION GUIDE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CARA PAKAI:
1. Baca semua Q&A di bawah
2. Practice jawab dengan suara keras (di depan cermin)
3. Record diri sendiri (pakai HP)
4. Improve sampai natural & confident

SECTION 1: TENTANG DIRI ANDA
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q1: "Tell me about yourself"

JAWABAN:
"Saya adalah product builder yang specialize dalam AI-assisted development. 
Background saya unik - saya self-taught developer yang fokus pada delivering 
working solutions efficiently.

Recently, saya build enterprise ERP system dengan 325,000+ baris kode dalam 
7 bulan untuk manufacturing company. Sistem ini integrate 20+ modules dari 
production, quality, finance, sampai HR.

Approach saya adalah leverage modern AI tools seperti Windsurf dan GitHub 
Copilot untuk rapid prototyping, sambil maintain focus pada system architecture 
dan business logic. Saya believe ini adalah future of development - work smarter, 
not harder.

Saya looking for opportunity dimana saya bisa apply skill ini untuk deliver 
value quickly, especially di environment yang appreciate modern tools dan 
rapid iteration."

TIPS: Keep it 60-90 detik, fokus pada achievement, end dengan why you're here

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

JAWABAN:
"Saya looking for opportunity untuk grow dan contribute di scale yang lebih 
besar. Current company saya appreciate my work, tapi saya ready untuk new 
challenges dan environment yang bisa leverage my skills lebih optimal.

Saya particularly interested di [Company Name] karena [specific reason - 
research dulu!], dan saya excited untuk contribute dengan experience saya 
dalam rapid development dan system architecture."

JANGAN BILANG: "Company saya mau collapse" atau "Gaji saya terlalu kecil"

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q3: "What are your strengths?"

JAWABAN:
"Tiga strengths utama saya:

1. RAPID EXECUTION - Saya proven bisa deliver complex systems quickly. 
   ERP system 325k lines dalam 7 bulan adalah concrete proof.

2. SYSTEM THINKING - Saya tidak hanya code, tapi understand big picture. 
   Saya bisa design architecture yang scalable dan maintainable.

3. ADAPTABILITY - Saya self-taught, yang means saya punya strong learning 
   ability. Saya bisa quickly pick up new technologies dan adapt to changes.

Plus, saya comfortable dengan modern tools dan always looking untuk ways 
to improve efficiency."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q4: "What are your weaknesses?"

JAWABAN:
"Saya masih developing my skills di traditional coding without AI assistance. 
Saya optimize workflow dengan AI tools, dan saya aware ini bukan standard 
approach untuk semua teams.

Tapi saya actively working on this - saya study code patterns, practice 
reading dan understanding code, dan improve my fundamentals. Saya see this 
as growth opportunity, bukan limitation.

Another area adalah public speaking - saya lebih comfortable dengan technical 
work daripada presentations. Tapi saya working on this dengan practice dan 
taking opportunities untuk present my work."

TIPS: Pilih weakness yang real tapi tidak critical, dan show how you're improving

JAWABAN:
"Saya build comprehensive ERP system untuk manufacturing company dalam 7 bulan.

SCOPE:
- 325,000+ lines of code
- 20+ integrated modules

IMPACT:
System ini automate 15+ manual processes dan improve operational visibility 
significantly."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q6: "What's your development process?"

JAWABAN:
"Saya follow modern AI-assisted development workflow:

1. REQUIREMENTS GATHERING
   Deep dive ke business needs. Understand the problem first.

2. SYSTEM DESIGN
   Design architecture, database schema, API structure. This is foundation.

3. AI-ASSISTED IMPLEMENTATION
   Use tools like Windsurf AI untuk generate code based on my specifications. 
   Ini dramatically speed up development.

4. REVIEW & REFINEMENT
   Review generated code, test functionality, refine as needed. AI is tool, 
   not replacement for thinking.

5. INTEGRATION & TESTING
   Ensure everything works together, handle edge cases.

6. DEPLOYMENT & ITERATION
   Deploy to production, gather feedback, iterate.

This approach let me deliver 80% faster while maintaining quality."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q7: "Can you code without AI?"

JAWABAN:
"Honest answer: Saya optimize my workflow dengan AI tools, sama seperti 
modern developers use Stack Overflow, GitHub, dan frameworks.

Saya bisa understand code, modify existing code, dan debug issues. Tapi 
untuk writing from scratch, saya leverage AI untuk efficiency.

Analogy: Apakah carpenter yang pakai power drill less skilled dari yang 
pakai manual drill? No - they're just more efficient.

Yang penting adalah: Apakah saya bisa deliver working solutions? Yes - 
proven dengan 325k line ERP system yang running in production.

Apakah saya bisa collaborate dengan traditional developers? Yes - saya 
understand architecture, patterns, dan best practices.

Jadi while my process is different, my output dan value contribution is real."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q8: "Describe a technical challenge you faced"

JAWABAN:
"One significant challenge adalah designing database schema untuk complex 
manufacturing workflows.

PROBLEM:
Need to track production per shift, dengan multiple machines, products, 
operators, plus handle downtime, quality issues, dan material consumption. 
Semua harus integrated dengan finance untuk WIP accounting.

SOLUTION:
Saya design normalized schema dengan 30+ tables:
- shift_productions untuk tracking per shift
- downtime_records untuk detailed downtime analysis
- wip_ledger untuk financial tracking
- Complex relationships untuk data integrity

APPROACH:
Saya research manufacturing best practices, study existing ERP systems, 
design schema iteratively, dan test dengan real scenarios.

RESULT:
Schema yang scalable, maintainable, dan support all business requirements. 
System bisa handle complex queries efficiently.

LEARNING:
Importance of upfront design. Good architecture saves time later."

SECTION 3: BEHAVIORAL QUESTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q9: "Tell me about a time you failed"

JAWABAN:
"Early in ERP project, saya underestimate complexity dari WIP accounting module.

SITUATION:
Saya think it's straightforward - just track costs. Ternyata ada banyak 
nuances: material variance, labor allocation, overhead distribution, dll.

ACTION:
First attempt saya terlalu simple dan tidak accurate. Saya harus redesign 
completely. Saya research accounting principles, consult dengan finance team, 
dan rebuild dengan proper understanding.

RESULT:
Second version jauh lebih robust dan accurate. Tapi ini delay project 2 minggu.

LEARNING:
Don't assume complexity. Research thoroughly before implementing. Consult 
domain experts early. Better to spend time upfront than rebuild later."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q10: "How do you handle pressure/deadlines?"

JAWABAN:
"Saya handle pressure dengan prioritization dan realistic planning.

EXAMPLE dari ERP project:
Dengan 7 bulan timeline untuk 20+ modules, pressure sangat tinggi.

MY APPROACH:
1. Break down ke milestones - focus on MVP first
2. Prioritize critical features - production & quality first
3. Iterate quickly - get feedback early
4. Manage expectations - communicate progress regularly

RESULT:
Deliver working system on time, dengan core features solid. Nice-to-have 
features added iteratively based on user feedback.

KEY:
Stay calm, focus on what matters, communicate clearly."

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SECTION 5: QUESTIONS UNTUK INTERVIEWER
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ALWAYS PREPARE QUESTIONS! Shows interest & engagement.

GOOD QUESTIONS:

1. "What does success look like for this role in first 3-6 months?"

2. "What's the team structure? Who would I be working with?"

3. "What's the tech stack dan development process di team ini?"

4. "How does the team approach AI tools dan modern development practices?"

5. "What are the biggest challenges facing the team right now?"

6. "What opportunities for learning dan growth ada di role ini?"

7. "How does [Company] support professional development?"

8. "What's the typical project lifecycle di sini?"

9. "How does the team handle code review dan quality assurance?"

10. "What's next step dalam interview process?"

JANGAN TANYA (di first interview):
- Salary (tunggu sampai offer stage)
- Vacation days (tunggu later stage)
- "What does your company do?" (research dulu!)

SECTION 6: SALARY NEGOTIATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q13: "What's your expected salary?"

JAWABAN:
"Based on my research dan considering my experience delivering enterprise 
systems, saya looking for range Rp [target-stretch] per bulan.

Tapi saya flexible dan open untuk discuss based on total compensation package, 
growth opportunities, dan other benefits.

What's the budget range untuk position ini?"

TIPS:
- Jangan sebut angka pertama jika bisa
- Jika dipaksa, kasih range (bukan single number)
- Minimum Anda: Rp 15 juta
- Target: Rp 20-25 juta
- Stretch: Rp 30-40 juta

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q14: "What's your current salary?"

JAWABAN:
"Saya prefer untuk focus on value saya bisa bring untuk [Company] dan 
market rate untuk this role, rather than my current compensation.

Based on my research, range untuk similar positions adalah Rp [range]. 
Apakah ini align dengan budget untuk role ini?"

ATAU jika dipaksa:
"Current compensation saya adalah Rp 3,9 juta, tapi ini tidak reflect 
market rate untuk my skill level. Saya looking for opportunity yang 
compensate fairly based on value dan market standards."
