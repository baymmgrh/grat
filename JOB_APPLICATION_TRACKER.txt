TIPS:
1. Apply pagi hari (8-10 AM) - higher response rate
2. Customize setiap application - jangan copy paste
3. Follow up setelah 1 minggu jika no response
4. Track semua di spreadsheet ini
5. Set realistic goals: 10 applications per week
6. Quality > Quantity - better 5 good applications than 20 bad ones
